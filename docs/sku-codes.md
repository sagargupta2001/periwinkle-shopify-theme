# SKU codes

The reference list of category codes used in SKUs (`PBS-<CODE>-<NNN>`).
`PBS` stands for **Periwinkle by Sherry**. The client assigns each number:
the next free number in her notebook for that category.

The client is sharing the full list. Until it arrives, only the codes she has
confirmed are marked as such.

| Code | Full form | Status | Numbering |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAR | Saree | Confirmed | Continues the existing sequence |
| SKZ | Salwar kameez | Confirmed | Continues. USS and SKR (a misspelling) are being folded in; see [sku-audit.md](sku-audit.md). |
| RED | Readymade | Confirmed | Not used yet |
| MIN | Miniatures | Confirmed | Continues |
| GIFT | Gifts | Confirmed | Not used yet |
| DUP | Dupatta | To confirm | **Restarted at 001 in November 2026.** The old numbers are retired; see [archive/dupattas-before-rebuild-2026-11.csv](archive/dupattas-before-rebuild-2026-11.csv). |
| EPJ | (jewellery, to confirm) | To confirm | — |
| JEW | (jewellery, to confirm) | To confirm | — |
| CMS | (ceramics, to confirm) | To confirm | — |

Retired codes: **USS** and **SKR**, both replaced by SKZ. "SKU" was also used
on some products as if it were a code. It was never a real code, and those
products are being renumbered.
